Getting started

In Survival, you gather resources, manage health and hunger, and protect yourself from hostile mobs. In Creative, you can fly and build with unlimited blocks. Choose a mode when creating your world.

  1. Break tree logs and turn them into planks in your inventory.
  2. Use planks to make a crafting table, then craft basic tools.
  3. Make a wooden pickaxe and mine stone for stronger stone tools.
  4. Build a shelter before nightfall and light it with torches.

Survival basics

  • Food: Gather crops, hunt animals, or fish. Cook food in a furnace for a useful supply.
  • Light: Make torches with sticks and coal or charcoal to illuminate your base and caves.
  • Rest: A bed lets you skip the night and sets your respawn point when you sleep.
  • Prepare: Carry food, a weapon, a pickaxe, and spare blocks when travelling.
  • Be careful: Watch your health, keep an eye out for mobs, and never dig straight down.

Exploring and progressing

Mark your route with landmarks or use a map so you can find your way back. Mine carefully and place torches as you go. Iron is a useful early resource for stronger tools and armor.

When ready for a bigger challenge, build a Nether portal with obsidian and light it with flint and steel. Bring armor, food, and supplies—the Nether is dangerous. You can also farm, trade with villagers, explore structures, or work toward finding the End.

Build a practical base

Start with a lit, enclosed room containing a bed, crafting table, furnace, and chests. Keep valuables organized, add windows, and fence off crops or animals. Expand and decorate as you collect more blocks.

Playing with friends

Bedrock multiplayer works across supported devices and worlds. Play with people you trust, agree on shared rules, and review privacy settings before joining a server.
